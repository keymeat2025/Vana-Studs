About Vana Studs

Rooted in Tamil Nadu's Equestrian Heritage

Founded in 2009 on the outskirts of Vellore, Vana Studs was born from a deep love for India's indigenous horse breeds. What started as a small riding school has grown into a full equestrian academy — breeding, boarding, and training riders of all ages.

We are one of the few centres in Tamil Nadu that actively preserves native horse lineages while offering internationally-aligned training programs for beginners and advanced riders alike.
